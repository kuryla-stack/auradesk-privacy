## 1. Introduction & Core Privacy Philosophy

AuraDesk: Daily Reflection ("AuraDesk", "we", "our", or "the App") is built on the principle of **absolute privacy and zero data harvesting**. We believe that your personal thoughts, daily reflections, mood logs, gratitude notes, and personal goals are deeply sacred and belong solely to you.

**AuraDesk operates 100% offline-first.** We do not operate user accounts, we do not have central servers storing your journal, and we do not track your behavior.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Data You Create in the App (Stored Locally Only)
When you use AuraDesk, you may create and store the following content on your local device:
* Daily reflections and structured answers
* Gratitude entries and custom notes
* Mood and energy check-in ratings and associated factor tags
* Personal goals, milestones, target dates, and checklist statuses
* Custom PIN lock credentials (hashed locally)
* App settings and theme preferences

**Where this data resides:**  
All of the above data is stored strictly in your device's private application sandbox (via local encrypted storage and SharedPreferences). It is **never** transmitted to our servers, third-party analytics services, or cloud providers.

### B. Data We DO NOT Collect
* ❌ No Personal Identifiable Information (no names, emails, phone numbers, addresses).
* ❌ No Location Data (no GPS, IP tracking, or Wi-Fi triangulation).
* ❌ No Device Identifiers (no advertising IDs, IMEI, or hardware serials).
* ❌ No Cloud Syncing without your explicit manual export.
* ❌ No Telemetry, Crash Analytics, or Tracking SDKs.
* ❌ No Third-Party Advertisements.

---

## 3. App Permissions

AuraDesk requires minimal device permissions to operate:
* **Storage / Share Sheet Access:** Only utilized when you explicitly trigger the "Export Backup (JSON)" or "Share Quote Card" features to share content via your operating system's native share sheet.
* **No Internet Permission Required:** The core functionality of AuraDesk does not require or depend on an active internet connection.

---

## 4. Data Security & PIN Protection

* **Local PIN Security:** You may enable an optional 4-digit PIN lock within the App. When activated, the PIN is validated locally to prevent unauthorized access by anyone who has physical possession of your device.
* **Manual Data Sovereignty:** You have complete control over your data. You can export your entire journal as a standard JSON file at any time, import backups, or permanently delete all data with a single tap via `Insights & Settings > Clear All Data`.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services & Libraries

AuraDesk does not incorporate third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising frameworks (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or AdMob). The application relies solely on standard open-source Flutter frameworks running client-side.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

AuraDesk does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal data is collected or transmitted from any user, the application is safe for all age groups.

---

## 7. Compliance with International Privacy Regulations

### A. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — EEA & UK)
Because all personal data is processed solely on your local device under your direct control, you maintain full data sovereignty (Right to Access, Right to Rectification, Right to Erasure, and Right to Data Portability via JSON export).

### B. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)
We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your personal information to third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.
